Royalties

For royalty payments, submit your invoice according to the royalty report you received.

PERIOD: S1 / S2 2026
DESCRIPTION: Royalty payment based on S1 2026 report for tracks included in AINSOF catalog.
AMOUNT: $XXX (must match the report)

Important

Your invoice must clearly specify what it relates to (project or royalty period), including all relevant details such as project title, project code, or reporting period.

Incomplete or unclear information may delay processing and payment.

Working with us

Paid in full, once

A buyout is one agreed payment for the work, invoiced per album or release. The rights move to AINSOF, and from there the music goes out across global content, media and brand campaigns. Where the deal is a hybrid, that payment sits alongside a royalty share rather than replacing it.

Every territory, every use

Royalties are administered through our sub-publishing network, territory by territory, so usage is reported and paid wherever in the world it happens. Each period you receive a report covering what was collected on your tracks, and you invoice against it — the period it covers, for the amount it states.

You write. We do the rest.

Artwork, video, marketing, mastering and your artist profile are all handled here. You write and produce; everything a release needs around the music is ours to build, so what reaches the catalogue is a finished record and not a folder of files.

Everywhere it can be heard

Releases are distributed and registered across the major streaming platforms, with an ISRC on every track and a UPC on every album. The catalogue is licensed to brands, agencies and productions worldwide, and every track is registered so its usage can be traced and reported.
